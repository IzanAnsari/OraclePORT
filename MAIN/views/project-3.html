Project Overview

I designed and developed advanced FRS reports in Oracle Fusion Cloud to support financial teams with real-time access to critical business data. Reports include standard Balance Sheets, Income Statements, and Trial Balances generated using General Ledger (GL) hierarchies and account groupings.

These reports utilized custom row and column sets, enabling dynamic structuring and financial visibility tailored to organizational needs. Each report incorporated account hierarchies, cost centers, business units, and currency conversions for multi-dimensional analysis.

Role-based data security was applied to ensure that users could access only relevant financial information. Reports were scheduled and published within Oracle Financial Reporting Center, empowering stakeholders with accurate and timely data for strategic planning and compliance.
